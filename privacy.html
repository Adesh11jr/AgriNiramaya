Privacy Policy

How AgriNiramaya handles user information.

This Privacy Policy explains how AgriNiramaya may collect, use and manage information when users interact with our platform.

1. Information We May Collect

The platform may collect information such as:

  • Name and email address.
  • Farm and crop details entered by users.
  • Plant images uploaded for disease analysis.
  • Soil readings received from IoT sensors.
  • Location information provided for agricultural analysis.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide plant health analysis, soil monitoring, agricultural suggestions and user-specific dashboard features.

3. Plant Images and Analysis

Uploaded plant images may be processed by the project's image analysis system to identify plants and detect possible diseases.

4. Data Security

Appropriate security measures should be implemented to protect user information from unauthorized access, modification or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Services

Some platform features may use external APIs or services. Their data handling practices may be governed by their own privacy policies.

6. User Responsibility

Users should provide accurate information and avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform develops and new features are introduced.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, users can visit our Contact Us page.
