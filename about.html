About AgriNiramaya

Healthy Plants. Smarter Farming.

Introduction

AgriNiramaya is an AI-enabled integrated plant health and soil monitoring system. It aims to combine image processing, IoT sensors and satellite-based mapping to provide useful agricultural information through a single digital platform.

Our Vision

Our vision is to support smarter and more informed agricultural practices by making plant health and soil-related information easier to access.

Project Objectives

  • Identify possible plant diseases through image analysis.
  • Monitor soil moisture, temperature and pH using IoT sensors.
  • Explore location-based agricultural information using satellite data.
  • Provide useful plant care and agricultural suggestions.
  • Integrate different monitoring features into one platform.

Key Features

  • Farm and crop information management.
  • AI-based plant disease detection.
  • IoT-based soil monitoring.
  • AI agriculture assistant.
  • Plant analysis and soil monitoring history.

Technology

The project uses web development technologies, Python-based backend development, image processing, IoT sensors and satellite-based geospatial information.
